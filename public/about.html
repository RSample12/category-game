Category Detectives

A free two-player deduction game. One phone, passed back and forth. Work out your opponent's secret suspect before they work out yours.

How to play

  1. Pick a category. Each player is secretly dealt one suspect from the same lineup.
  2. On your turn, ask your opponent one yes/no question out loud about their suspect.
  3. Use the answer to rule suspects out on your board, or accuse the one you think they hold. You can't do both in the same turn.
  4. A correct accusation wins the round. A wrong one rules that suspect out and ends your turn.
  5. Rematch to keep a running score, or switch to a new category.

Good to know

  • Free to play, with no account and no ads.
  • Rule-outs can be undone until you end your turn.
