Guesswork

A free deduction game for 2–4 players. One phone, passed around the table. Work out your target's secret pick before someone works out yours.

How to play

  1. Pick a category. Each player is secretly dealt one card from the same lineup.
  2. On your turn, ask your opponent one yes/no question out loud about their pick.
  3. Use the answer to rule cards out on your board, or guess the one you think they picked. You can't do both in the same turn.
  4. A correct guess wins the round. A wrong one rules that card out and ends your turn.
  5. Rematch with the same lineup, or switch to a new category.

Good to know

  • Free to play, with no account and no ads.
  • Rule-outs can be undone until you end your turn.
